# Marmar resume style

Use Marmar's direct request and the target job posting to choose emphasis. For career facts, read `/Users/marmar/job-search-agent/data/career_profile_source_of_truth.md` when available. Treat older resumes as examples of phrasing or appearance, not as authority when facts conflict. Do not carry forward the 2013 address, email, dates, or roles merely because they appear in the style example. Use current contact details supplied for the application.

## Preferred structure

1. Center `MARYAM ZARE`, then location and a concise contact line in the document body.
2. Use one short, uppercase headline describing her real positioning for the target role.
3. Order sections as `Skills`, `Experience`, `Education`. Keep certifications at the end of Education when relevant.
4. In Experience, put **role title and dates on the first line**, then **employer and location on the second line**, followed by compact square bullets. Use the confirmed canonical job titles and dates from the career profile.
5. Keep Skills as a short, scannable list of actual capabilities, not a prose summary. Introduce a separate Summary only when Marmar requests one or it materially helps a specific application without crowding the page.

## Voice and tailoring

Write like an experienced person describing work to another person: direct verbs, concrete problems, decisions, collaborators, and outcomes. Show how her platform, analytics, engineering-management, and partner experience solves the target team's problems. Translate adjacent experience honestly; never claim a named tool, domain, certification, hands-on build role, or ROI figure without support. Keep distinct metrics separate as required by the career profile. Do not merge influence across teams with direct-report counts.

## Visual treatment and file quality

Preserve the recognizable 2013-inspired appearance: Georgia-like serif body, centered name, muted sage/gray-green headline band, restrained section headings, green-gray employer lines, and compact square bullets. The headline must be an ordinary body paragraph with shading, not a table or text box. Keep all important information in body paragraphs; no populated header or footer. Use one column and readable spacing. Aim for one page when content permits, but prefer a balanced two-page resume over cramped type or an orphaned second page. Render and visually inspect every page before delivery.

When working in the job-search-agent repository, its `resume-one` pipeline has its own validated output rules. If using that pipeline, follow its requirements and explain any unavoidable difference from this visual preference. Do not silently change Marmar's preferred section order just to imitate a generic ATS convention.
